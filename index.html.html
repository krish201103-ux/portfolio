About me

I’m a biotechnology student with a strong interest in AI/ML, computational biology and data analytics. I enjoy turning messy biological data into something people can act on, from epidemic forecasting to imaging-based biomarkers.

I’ve built AI-driven healthcare prototypes, worked in a systems biology data analytics lab, and done research using machine learning on biological data. Alongside the technical work, I lead and coordinate teams: as a class representative, a club secretary and an events coordinator.
